# Supplementary Results — exploratory cohort associations

Beyond the pre-registered confirmatory pairs, the cohort frames were swept for
all exposure x outcome pairs actually available (68 pairs analysed). Three
directions recurred across cohorts and are recorded here as exploratory only,
since they were not pre-registered:

- diabetes with moderate and severe vision impairment (KLoSA OR 1.50 and 1.99;
  SHARE severe 1.46);
- diabetes with self-reported glaucoma (LASI OR 1.64, q = 1.2 x 10^-10);
- current smoking with severe vision impairment (HRS OR 1.98, q = 8.1 x 10^-20).

All three point the same way as r20 and are consistent with a metabolic and
smoking-related contribution to vision loss; none is offered as a confirmed
finding.
